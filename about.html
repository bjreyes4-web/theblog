WHO AM I?

Hello world! I’m Brenda Reyes, a Design major / Business minor student at USF!

Thrifting has always been something I have done to feel more comfortable in myself. When I was younger, that’s where I would find clothes that would fit me well and were sometimes cuter and more flattering than retail options.

Even now, I still love finding vintage pieces & accessories. It’s also a more sustainable alternative to fast fashion.

This Paris trip was such a special experience, and thrifting there made it even better.

I hope I can help you on your own thrifting journey!
